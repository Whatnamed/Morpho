This fosters independence, collaboration, and sustainable, local economic growth. In many parts of the world, especially in economically disadvantaged regions, accessing essential goods can be a major challenge and is often linked to dependence on imports. Tando emerged from exchanges with a Tanzanian doctor, highlighting the need for affordable, locally producible solutions. Initially addressing specific issues in Tanzania, Tando has evolved into a globally scalable open-source concept. Tando In economically disadvantaged regions like Tanzania, most people own a smart- phone, making an online platform widely accessible. The open-source app of Tando allows customization and active contributions to its features and design. Users can search for available constructions or create enquiries for specific items. They can also share pictures and reviews on Tando‘s goods. Registered providers can accept enquiries, showcase their services or offers, and connect with other stakeholders via a map feature or community chats to get certain components, tools or services additionally needed. Unlike imports, Tando‘s goods are cheaper and repairable as the components are modular and spare parts are easier to get. This enables the creation of diverse constructions, from hospital inventory, home furniture, transportation devices like wheelbarrows or trailers to sports and leisure equipment. Visual instruction manuals make the production practical and accessible, opening a new field of job opportunities, also for illiterates. Developers can refine the software, improve instruction manuals, and share new designs. The platform fosters collaboration through community group chats to exchange on specific topics and support each other. Tando could additionally expand to social media, enabling broader sharing of new ideas, networking and visibility. Through crowdfunding, community projects such as the construction of public sports stations or playgrounds can be realised, ajusted to the region‘s conditions.
