The workspace button, in the three variants the product ships.

\`\`\`jsx
<Button variant="brand" icon={<Icon name="download" size={14} />}>输出</Button>
<Button icon={<Icon name="import" size={14} />}>导入</Button>
<Button variant="icon" icon={<Icon name="search" />} title="搜索" />
\`\`\`

One \`brand\` button per surface — in the workspace that is 输出, in the AI panel it is the confirm action. Labels are verb-first and two characters where possible (导入 / 归档 / 输出).
